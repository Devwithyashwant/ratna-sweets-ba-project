# Requirement Elicitation
## Ratna Sweets — Festival Demand & Freshness Management System

*Note: These interviews are simulated for this portfolio case study, based on realistic personas and common operational patterns in this business type. They are not real transcripts.*

## Interview Guide (used across stakeholders)

1. Walk me through how you currently plan for festival demand (e.g. Diwali).
2. What information do you wish you had, but don't currently get?
3. Where do you see the biggest waste — of product, time, or money?
4. What's the one thing that, if fixed, would make your job significantly easier?
5. How do you currently share information with other departments/outlets?

---

## Simulated Interview: Lakshmi Nair, Production Manager

> "Sales tells me about a big bulk order maybe two days before Diwali, after I've already finalized the week's production schedule. I either scramble to add an extra shift, or I say no and we lose the order. I don't get any early signal — not even a rough estimate — from outlet sales data."

**Key pain point:** No early demand signal → reactive production planning → overtime costs or lost orders.

---

## Simulated Interview: Arjun Reddy, Inventory Manager

> "Each outlet calls in or WhatsApps me their stock count once a day, if I'm lucky. I have no idea what's sitting near its expiry date until a store manager tells me they had to throw out a tray of peda. By then it's too late to do anything — like move it to another outlet or discount it."

**Key pain point:** No real-time, batch-level stock/expiry visibility across outlets → wastage discovered too late to act.

---

## Simulated Interview: Priya Menon, Sales Manager

> "During Dussehra last year, two of our outlets ran out of dry-fruit barfi by the second day, while another outlet had extra stock sitting unsold. I knew it was happening in real time but had no way to trigger a transfer — by the time anyone acted, the festival rush was over."

**Key pain point:** Demand mismatch across outlets, no mechanism to rebalance stock mid-festival.

---

## Simulated Interview: Deepa Krishnan, Finance Manager

> "I can tell you our overall festival revenue, but I can't tell you how much we lost to wastage, or whether a given outlet's overproduction was worth the extra sales elsewhere. Every year leadership asks 'was this festival season profitable after wastage?' and I can't give a confident answer."

**Key pain point:** No cost-of-wastage visibility → can't evaluate ROI of operational decisions.

---

## Simulated Interview: Ramesh Iyer, Business Owner

> "I want to open two more outlets next year, but honestly, I'm nervous — if we can't manage demand and freshness across 5 outlets today, I don't see how we scale to 7 without this chaos getting worse. I need a single view of what's happening across the business, not five different phone calls."

**Key pain point:** No consolidated operational visibility → scaling risk.
