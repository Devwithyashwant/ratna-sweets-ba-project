# Stakeholder Register
## Ratna Sweets — Festival Demand & Freshness Management System

| Stakeholder | Persona | Primary Interest | Key Concern | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business Owner | Mr. Ramesh Iyer | Overall profitability & brand reputation | Wastage eating into margins; wants data-backed decisions, not gut feel | High |
| Production Manager | Lakshmi Nair | Smooth, predictable production runs | Gets demand numbers too late to plan raw material & labor; overtime costs spike at festivals | High |
| Inventory Manager | Arjun Reddy | Accurate stock visibility across all locations | No real-time view of what's at each outlet; can't tell what's near expiry until it's too late | Medium |
| Sales Manager | Priya Menon | Meeting customer demand, especially bulk festival orders | Outlets run out of popular items mid-festival while others overstock; loses walk-in sales | High |
| Procurement Manager | Suresh Pillai | Timely, cost-effective raw material purchase | Orders raw materials based on guesswork since there's no consolidated demand signal | Medium |
| Store Manager (x5 outlets) | Various | Smooth daily operations at outlet level | Gets stock allocations that don't match actual local demand patterns | Medium |
| Finance Manager | Deepa Krishnan | Cost control, wastage reduction | No visibility into wastage cost by product/outlet; can't quantify ROI of any fix | High |
| Development/IT Support | External vendor | Feasible, well-specified system to build | Needs clear, unambiguous requirements to avoid rework | Low (but critical for delivery) |
| QA Tester | External/internal | Verifying the solution actually meets requirements | Needs testable, traceable requirements | Low |

**Note:** All personas above are simulated for this portfolio case study — Ratna Sweets is a fictional business created to demonstrate the full BA lifecycle.
