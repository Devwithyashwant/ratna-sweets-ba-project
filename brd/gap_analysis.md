# SWOT & Gap Analysis
## Ratna Sweets — Current State (As-Is)

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- Strong brand loyalty and repeat customers for festival purchases
- Skilled production staff with deep product knowledge
- Five physical outlets giving reasonable market coverage

**Weaknesses**
- No shared system between production, inventory, and outlets — everything runs on phone calls and WhatsApp
- Demand signals reach production too late to act on
- No visibility into near-expiry stock until it's already wasted
- No way to rebalance stock between outlets mid-festival

**Opportunities**
- A structured demand-planning process could reduce wastage and stockouts simultaneously
- Data from this system could support the owner's expansion plans with actual evidence, not guesswork
- Early demand visibility could reduce overtime/labor cost spikes

**Threats**
- Competitors with better-planned festival operations could capture lost sales during stockouts
- Continued wastage directly erodes thin margins on perishable goods
- Scaling to more outlets without fixing this first will likely multiply the current problems

### Gap Analysis — Current State vs. Desired State

| Area | Current State | Desired State | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand visibility | Reactive, word-of-mouth, arrives late | Early, data-informed demand estimates ahead of festivals | No structured forecasting process |
| Inventory visibility | Manual daily call-ins, outlet-by-outlet | Real-time, batch-level stock visibility across all outlets | No shared inventory system |
| Freshness monitoring | Discovered only when a store manager notices | Proactive alerts before products near expiry | No expiry tracking mechanism |
| Cross-outlet rebalancing | Not possible mid-festival | Ability to shift stock from surplus to shortage outlets | No mechanism or visibility to trigger transfers |
| Cost of wastage | Unknown, not tracked | Tracked by product/outlet, tied to financial reporting | No wastage cost tracking |
| Management oversight | Owner relies on phone calls for a sense of the business | Single consolidated operational view | No dashboard/reporting layer |
