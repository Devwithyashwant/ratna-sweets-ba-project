# Project Charter
## Festival Demand & Freshness Management System

**Project Sponsor:** Business Owner / Management, Ratna Sweets
**Project Role:** Business Analyst
**Business Domain:** Food Manufacturing and Retail (Indian Sweets)
**Project Type:** Business Process Improvement and Information System Design

---

### 1. Business Context

Ratna Sweets is a premium Indian sweets manufacturer operating one central manufacturing unit and five retail outlets across the city. The business produces traditional sweets (ladoo, barfi, peda, dry-fruit sweets) and festival gift boxes, with sharp demand spikes during Diwali, Dussehra, and Raksha Bandhan.

### 2. Business Need

The organisation currently manages production and inventory using manual registers and disconnected spreadsheets across outlets. This results in inaccurate production planning, inconsistent inventory visibility, and no structured way to track product freshness — especially during festival periods when demand is highly volatile.

### 3. Project Goal

Analyse existing business operations and define a proposed solution that improves demand visibility, production planning, inventory management, and product freshness monitoring — for both regular and festival demand cycles.

### 4. Project Objectives

- Understand current demand and production planning practices across the manufacturing unit and five outlets
- Identify inventory and freshness management challenges
- Document stakeholder and system requirements
- Design improved business workflows (as-is → to-be)
- Define reporting and monitoring requirements
- Establish measurable business performance indicators

### 5. Project Scope

**In Scope**
- Demand analysis and forecasting requirements
- Production planning requirements
- Product and batch-level inventory tracking (manufacturing unit + 5 outlets)
- Shelf-life and expiry monitoring
- Advance and bulk order management (e.g. festival gift box pre-orders)
- Management reporting and KPI dashboards

**Out of Scope**
- Actual manufacturing machinery automation
- Live integration with external ERP systems
- Payment gateway development
- Deployment of a production-ready commercial application

### 6. Expected Business Benefits

- Improved visibility into product demand across outlets
- Better production planning aligned to festival cycles
- Reduced risk of excess and insufficient inventory
- Improved freshness monitoring and reduced wastage
- Better order fulfilment visibility for bulk/advance orders
- More informed management decisions backed by data

### 7. Key Constraints and Assumptions

- Historical sales, production, and inventory data is not digitally available; **synthetic but realistic data will be used** for this analysis, clearly labeled wherever used
- Stakeholder interviews are simulated, based on realistic personas for this business type, and are clearly labeled as simulated
- Proposed benefits will be evaluated against a defined baseline rather than assumed

### 8. Success Criteria

This project is complete when: business requirements are documented, current and proposed workflows are modelled, analytical evidence is prepared from the synthetic dataset, the proposed solution is validated through UAT scenarios, and final recommendations are presented to management via a stakeholder deck.
